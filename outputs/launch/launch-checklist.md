# Northstar Tech Concierge — practical launch checklist

Owner: Sean Widner · (352) 875-0467 · Company Gmail: [CREATE COMPANY GMAIL]

## Offer and operations

- [ ] Choose the launch name, service area, hours, response window, and appointment formats.
- [ ] Replace every placeholder in the service menu, intake flow, email templates, and card artwork.
- [ ] Read the pricing-and-scope guide and add its boundaries to the customer agreement and intake script.
- [ ] Set three starter prices, a travel rule, cancellation window, and scope-change process.
- [ ] Create a simple booking route and a secure place for minimal customer notes.
- [ ] Prepare a one-page session recap template and a referral list for out-of-scope requests.

## Trust and readiness

- [ ] Use a dedicated business email and phone/voicemail greeting.
- [ ] Turn on multi-factor authentication for business accounts; keep recovery details offline and secure.
- [ ] Test the booking link, contact form, phone, QR code, and automated confirmations.
- [ ] Draft plain-language privacy, cancellation, and payment information appropriate to your location; get professional advice where needed.
- [ ] Decide how payments and receipts will work; confirm local tax and registration requirements with a qualified professional.

## Public presence

- [ ] Publish the website with one clear promise, service menu, service area, and booking CTA.
- [ ] Add the business card front/back as editable source files and export print-ready versions after final review.
- [ ] Claim consistent social handles and add the same name, description, phone, and URL.
- [ ] Ask three trusted people to test the website on a phone and complete a mock booking.
- [ ] Prepare a launch announcement and a helpful first post (a small, safe tech tip).

## First 30 days

- [ ] Contact 10 warm connections and invite questions, not hard sells.
- [ ] Track inquiries, booked sessions, repeat questions, time spent, and referrals in a simple sheet.
- [ ] Send a recap after every appointment and request a testimonial only with permission.
- [ ] Review pricing and scope weekly; update the FAQ from real customer language.
- [ ] Reserve one block each week for admin, learning, and improving the onboarding flow.
