## The access rate

Northstar reserves a small number of appointments each month at reduced rates for people who would benefit from a lower-cost starting point. This is a limited access program, not a promise of unlimited discounted service.

No proof or private financial details are required. A customer can simply say, “I’d like to use the access rate.” Sean may pause the offer when the monthly allocation is full and can offer the next available standard-rate appointment instead.

### Quick Fix limits

- Remote only, one simple question or setting check, one device, and 15 minutes maximum.
- No passwords, one-time codes, financial accounts, medical records, legal documents, money movement, emergency support, malware response, data recovery, or multi-step setup.
- If the issue needs more time, access, or risk than the slot allows, stop and recommend a standard session or qualified specialist. Do not continue without a new quote and approval.

## Simple rules

- One access-rate appointment per household per calendar month.
- Access rates apply to the listed scope and time only; extra time, travel, new devices, or a new problem require approval at the applicable rate.
- Access rates cannot be combined with another coupon, referral credit, same-day fee waiver, or other promotion.
- Access appointments are scheduled during regular hours and are not emergency, after-hours, or rush service.
- The same consent, privacy, payment, cancellation, and “will not handle” boundaries apply at every price.
- If the access allocation is full, offer the next available standard-rate appointment or a referral; do not ask for sensitive financial documents.
- A customer may decline the access rate and book at the standard rate without explanation.

## Customer-facing wording

> Need a lower-cost starting point? Northstar keeps a limited number of monthly access-rate appointments at reduced prices. No proof or private financial details are required—just ask when you reach out. Access appointments have the same clear scope, privacy boundaries, and customer control as every other session.

## What the discount never changes

The access rate does not expand the service scope. Northstar still does not access or manage financial accounts, payment credentials, medical records, legal or identity documents, passwords, one-time codes, money movement, surveillance tools, or unauthorized systems. Northstar also does not provide legal, tax, medical, financial, or regulated cybersecurity advice.
