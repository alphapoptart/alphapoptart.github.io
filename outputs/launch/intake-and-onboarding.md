## 1. Inquiry reply

Respond within [RESPONSE WINDOW]. Ask for the customer’s goal, device(s), preferred format, availability, and any accessibility or privacy concerns. Do not request passwords by email or text.

## 2. Fit check (10 minutes)

Confirm:

- The desired result and the smallest useful appointment.
- Device type, operating system if known, and whether the customer can be present.
- Remote, in-person, or phone/video preference.
- Service area, price range, timing, and any travel needs.
- Whether a referral to the device maker, internet provider, or another specialist is more appropriate.

## 3. Booking confirmation

Send the appointment date/time, duration, price or estimate, location/link, cancellation window, and a short preparation list. State that work is customer-approved, customer-present, and scope may be paused for confirmation.

## 4. Pre-appointment checklist

Ask the customer to have the device charged, relevant cables available, and account recovery methods accessible. Ask them to close unrelated private material. Never ask them to send passwords or security codes.

## 5. Session structure

1. Restate the goal and success signal.
2. Inspect only what is needed; explain before changing anything.
3. Make one change at a time and confirm the customer’s choice.
4. Check the result together.
5. Summarize what changed, what to watch for, and the next step.

## 6. Closeout

Send a brief recap within [FOLLOW-UP WINDOW]: work completed, customer actions, open questions, and the next appointment if requested. Record only the minimum operational notes needed for follow-up; store them securely and follow your local privacy obligations.

## 7. Intake form fields

Name, preferred contact, goal in their words, device(s), location/remote preference, availability, accessibility needs, urgency, prior troubleshooting tried, “anything I should avoid changing?”, consent to receive appointment messages, and referral/notes.
