This mini system is designed to feel calm, practical, and human: a trusted local guide for people who want technology to work without a project-management burden.

## Positioning

**One-line promise:** Friendly, patient tech help for home, work, and the moments in between.

**Voice:** Clear, warm, reassuring, capable. Explain the “why” in plain language; never shame the customer for not knowing a setting.

**Avoid:** Fear-based security claims, guarantees, “we fix everything” language, or regulated advice. Describe what is included and what is not.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Ink / headings | Deep navy | `#101820` |
| Primary accent | Helpful teal | `#18A999` |
| Highlight | Warm yellow | `#F5B841` |
| Paper / background | Soft white | `#F7F8F4` |
| Secondary text | Slate | `#40525A` |

Use deep navy for high-contrast headings, teal for actions and rules, and yellow sparingly for reassurance or emphasis. Keep generous whitespace.

## Type and layout

- Web-safe type: Arial or Helvetica for clean, accessible rendering.
- Headings: bold, sentence case; body copy: regular weight and short paragraphs.
- Use rounded cards, simple line icons, and one clear call to action per screen.
- Keep a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text.

## Messaging examples

- “Bring your question. We’ll make a plan.”
- “Patient help for the devices you already own.”
- “A calmer setup, one small win at a time.”

Before publishing, replace every bracketed placeholder in the business card and launch documents. Confirm service area, pricing, availability, and booking details are accurate.
