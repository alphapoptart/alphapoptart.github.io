Hi, I’m Sean.

I started Villages Tech Concierge for one simple reason: too many of my neighbors here in The Villages were being talked over, talked down to, or left waiting on hold when all they wanted was for their technology to work.

I come to your home, sit down with your devices, and fix things at a pace that’s comfortable for you. There are no silly questions here — if you want to know why something happened or how to do it yourself next time, I’ll happily walk you through it. If you’d rather just have it working and get back to your day, that’s fine too.

Along the way, I’ll always keep an eye out for your safety. Sadly, scammers target folks our age more than any other group. Every visit includes plain-English tips on spotting phone scams, fake pop-ups, and the new wave of AI-powered fraud — so you can use today’s technology with confidence instead of worry.

What I believe

  • Patience first. We go at your speed, and we repeat anything as many times as you like.
  • Honesty always. If a five-minute fix is all you need, a five-minute fix is what you’ll get.
  • Your home, your rules. I treat your home and your privacy with the same respect I’d want for my own family.
  • No jargon. Plain English is the only language spoken here.

When I’m not untangling Wi-Fi, you’ll find me hosting free scam-defense coffee talks around The Villages — come say hello!
