Who is Northstar for?+

Northstar is for households, independent professionals, and small teams who want practical, patient help with everyday technology.

Do you work remotely?+

Yes. Many check-ins can happen remotely, and we’ll discuss the best format for your situation during the first call.

How does pricing work?+

We scope the work before we start and share a clear estimate. For ongoing support, we can discuss a simple recurring arrangement.

Can you help with passwords and accounts?+

We can guide you through safer account practices and setup. You keep control of your credentials; we never need your passwords sent by email or text.

What work do you not handle?+

We do not access financial accounts, medical records, legal or identity documents, money movement, passwords, one-time codes, surveillance tools, or unauthorized systems. We also do not provide legal, tax, medical, financial, or regulated cybersecurity advice.
